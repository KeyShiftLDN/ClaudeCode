## What we offer: the Precision Streamline System (three moves)
1. **Prepare: Precision Training.** Self-paced online training for mentors, coaches, youth workers, pastoral leads and parents.
   - Level 1: foundations. Free, instant access, no card. Certificate of completion.
   - Level 2: live delivery and safeguarding. £34.99 one-off per person. Certificate.
   - Facilitator and cohort options on enquiry.
2. **Process: PILLAR coaching.** Identity-first coaching with a KSL coach. Route to Certified Precision Practitioner. Foundation 6h, Formation 8h, Framework 10h; 1:1 or cohort. Free PILLAR Diagnostic available.
3. **PRE:CORD.** Platform to record every session, track each young person's progress and report impact.
   - Young people get structured, identity-first support.
   - Mentors get ready-to-deliver tools.
   - Organisations get live safeguarding oversight built in.
   - Commissioners and funders get real-time evidence they can report.
   - 30-day free trial, no card needed.

## The method: SHAPE model
Five areas of a young person's life: **S**chool, **H**ome, **A**spirations, **P**erspectives, **E**xperiences. "We target the whole life, not the symptom."

## Why it matters (the pain we solve)
- Youth interventions are often short, unmeasured and disconnected.
- Activity gets counted; impact gets assumed.
- Evidence sits in someone else's filing cabinet, so funder reports are painful.
- Practitioners and volunteers work in isolation without a shared method.
- Funding cycles end and the work disappears with them.

## Angles by audience
- **Youth charities:** add structure and evidence to the work you already do, and report outcomes funders trust. Free Level 1 gets the whole team, including volunteers, on one method.
- **Sports academies and clubs:** develop the whole athlete. A structured off-pitch identity and life-skills pathway players own, alongside performance. Relevant to player welfare and safeguarding leads.

## Buying triggers to look for
- Hiring youth workers, mentors, programme coordinators or a safeguarding / player welfare lead.
- New funding announced, or a funding bid or grant report season coming up.
- New mentoring, outreach or community programme launched.
- Site mentions impact reports, outcomes or "measuring impact" but shows only activity numbers.
